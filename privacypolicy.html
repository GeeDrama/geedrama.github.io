This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use GeeDrama: Short Drama Series, our Android and iOS applications, this website, and related support and purchase services (the "Service"). In this policy, "GeeDrama", "we", "us", and "our" refer to the provider of the GeeDrama service.

1. Scope

GeeDrama is a short drama entertainment service. This policy applies to information processed in connection with the Service. The information involved depends on the features you use, your device settings, and your choices.

Google Play, the Apple App Store, and other third-party services operate under their own privacy policies. This policy does not replace those policies.

2. Information We Process

Information you provide

  • Account details, where an account is used: Account identifiers, sign-in details supplied by your login provider, email address, profile information you choose to provide, and account settings.
  • Support and feedback: Your contact details, messages, and any account, device, transaction, screenshots, or other information you choose to send when requesting help.

Information associated with use of the app

  • Device and technical information: Device type, operating system, app version, language, network information, IP address, and diagnostic or error information needed to operate and troubleshoot the Service.
  • Viewing and interaction information: The dramas and episodes you open, playback progress, viewing history, and interactions with the app's available features.
  • Purchase information: Product and order identifiers, purchase or subscription status, refunds, and records of the content or benefits available to you. Where coins or rewards are offered, this can include balance changes, unlock records, and reward status.
  • Advertising information, where ads are enabled: Ad impressions, clicks, reward completion, device or advertising identifiers where permitted, and measurement or fraud-prevention signals.

Information from service partners

Google Play and the Apple App Store can provide transaction and entitlement information needed to verify purchases, subscriptions, and refunds. Content, playback, analytics, diagnostics, advertising, and technical service partners can provide information related to the functions they perform for the Service.

Only provide information that is needed for your request. Do not send passwords, full payment card details, or unrelated sensitive information to support.

3. How Information Is Used

  • Provide and maintain short drama playback and the features you use.
  • Maintain viewing progress and account-related preferences where supported.
  • Verify purchases, deliver paid entitlements, and handle transaction issues.
  • Administer coins, episode unlocks, subscriptions, or rewards where offered.
  • Deliver and measure advertising where enabled, subject to applicable choices and consent requirements.
  • Analyze performance, diagnose errors, improve the Service, and prevent fraud or misuse.
  • Respond to support inquiries and privacy requests.
  • Provide service-related communications and notifications where enabled.
  • Comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the rights and safety of users and the Service.

Where consent is required for particular processing, it must be obtained before that processing takes place. You may withdraw consent as described below.

4. When Information Is Shared

Information can be shared with the following categories of recipients when needed for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Operational service providers: Hosting, storage, support, diagnostics, analytics, security, and other technical providers.
  • Content and playback partners: Providers that deliver drama content, playback, availability information, and related technical services.
  • Payment platforms: Google Play, the Apple App Store, and transaction-verification services involved in purchases, refunds, and entitlement management.
  • Advertising and measurement partners: Where advertising is enabled, providers involved in ad delivery, rewards, measurement, and fraud prevention.
  • Legal or business recipients: Authorities or professional advisers where necessary to comply with law, address security incidents, protect rights, or support a business transfer, subject to applicable safeguards.

If processing is considered a sale, sharing, or targeted advertising under applicable privacy law, you may have a right to opt out. See "Your Privacy Rights" below.

5. Payments and Entitlements

Paid content and any purchase options are described in the app before purchase. Google Play processes purchases made through Google Play, and Apple processes purchases made through the App Store. We receive the transaction information needed to verify and deliver your purchase. We do not receive your full payment card number or your Google or Apple Account password through these store billing systems.

Transaction, refund, coin, unlock, and subscription records, where relevant, help provide purchased benefits, resolve issues, and prevent fraud. Payment, renewal, cancellation, and refund terms are explained in the Terms of Use and the purchase interface.

6. Advertising and Device Choices

Where the app includes advertising, advertising partners may process device identifiers and information about ad interactions for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. Optional rewarded ads, if offered, also require completion and eligibility checks before rewards can be credited.

You can manage available advertising privacy controls through your Android, Google, or iOS settings, and through in-app privacy controls where available. Where applicable, iOS tracking permissions can be managed in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Choices and consent requirements vary by location and device. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily remove all ads.

7. This Website and Local Storage

This website stores your selected display language in your browser under the key geedrama-language. This lets the site remember your language on a later visit. You can clear that preference by clearing site data in your browser.

The website itself does not include advertising or analytics scripts. Hosting infrastructure may process ordinary connection information, such as IP addresses, browser details, requested pages, and timestamps, to deliver and protect the site. Links to app stores, the account deletion portal, or an email application take you to services with their own privacy practices.

8. Your Choices and Account Deletion

You can manage device permissions and notification settings through Android or iOS settings. Where the app offers account, history, or privacy controls, you can also use those controls.

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data, or request deletion of eligible personal data without deleting your account. See the account and data deletion page for instructions.

Email geedrama.feedback@outlook.com for privacy requests, account deletion, purchases, or other support. We may request limited information to verify that a request relates to your account. Do not send account passwords.

Deleting an account or uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. Manage subscriptions separately through Google Play subscription settings or Apple subscription settings, according to the store where you purchased them.

9. Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as needed for the purpose for which it is processed, including providing the Service, delivering purchases, handling support, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations.

Account and viewing data can be deleted following a verified request, subject to applicable exceptions. Transaction records may need to be retained for accounting, tax, refund, or fraud-prevention purposes. Security and diagnostic records are kept for the period needed to investigate and address relevant issues. Where information is retained after a deletion request, retention is limited to the applicable purpose or legal requirement.

Information is deleted or de-identified when it is no longer needed. Any retained copies remain subject to applicable deletion requirements and are not used to reactivate a deleted account.

10. Security

Safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information and the risks of processing are used to protect the Service. Access to information should be limited to authorized personnel and providers who need it for their work.

No system is completely secure. Please protect your device and login credentials, and contact support if you believe your account or information has been compromised.

11. International Processing

The Service may use providers in countries other than the country where you live. Their data protection laws may differ from those in your location. Where cross-border transfer requirements apply, applicable safeguards and legal transfer mechanisms must be used.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information; restrict or object to processing; withdraw consent; or opt out of certain processing, including targeted advertising, sale, or sharing. You may also have a right to appeal a decision on a privacy request and to contact your local data protection authority.

Email geedrama.feedback@outlook.com for privacy requests, account deletion, purchases, or other support. Requests are handled in accordance with the procedures and time periods required by applicable law. Identity verification may be necessary, and any lawful exception will be explained where required. You will not be discriminated against for exercising applicable privacy rights.

EEA and UK

Where applicable, processing relies on performance of a contract, compliance with legal obligations, consent, or legitimate interests, depending on the purpose. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests and withdraw consent without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

California and other U.S. states

Applicable rights may include access, correction, deletion, portability, and opting out of sale, sharing, or targeted advertising. An authorized agent may make a request where permitted, subject to verification requirements.

13. Children and Age Requirements

GeeDrama is not directed to children. Users must meet the age requirements applicable to the Service in their location. Where parental or guardian permission is required by law, that permission must be obtained before use.

If you believe a child has provided personal information without the required permission, contact geedrama.feedback@outlook.com. Information collected in violation of applicable requirements will be addressed and deleted as required.

14. Third-Party Services

Third-party websites, app stores, payment providers, and other services have their own terms and privacy policies. For information about store services, see Google's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

15. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the Service or applicable requirements change. The date above identifies the current version. Material changes will be communicated where required, and any additional consent required for new processing must be obtained separately.
